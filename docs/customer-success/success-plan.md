# Success plan - onboarding to value review

> Fictional 30/60/90-day lifecycle. Proposed phases, not completed work or elapsed customer history. Lab execution can take place sooner with actual dates recorded separately.

| Phase | Goal | Actions and owner | Evidence / exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 0-30: onboarding | First useful experience | CSM confirms brief/owners; technical owner deploys; support lead explains status and escalation | Endpoint evidence, onboarding record, limitations and escalation route |
| Days 31-60: adoption | Use the portal without avoidable blockers | CSM checks learning gaps; support lead practises an incident; technical owner fixes issues | Walkthrough, incident timeline, customer update and evidence-based health assessment |
| Days 61-90: value | Continue, change or pause | CSM reviews goals/risks with sponsor; technical owner reviews costs | Evidence-backed review, unresolved risks, decision owner and next checkpoint |

## Onboarding checklist

- [ ] Luca explains and adjusts the discovery brief.
- [ ] Agree lab scope, access owner and budget before provisioning.
- [ ] Follow the [deployment guide](../deployment-guide.md), checking current portal settings.
- [ ] Verify the public portal and API; capture evidence.
- [ ] Explain simulated, point-in-time and unconfigured checks.
- [ ] Walk through one runbook and the escalation route.
- [ ] Record questions and training needs; do not invent satisfaction feedback.
- [ ] Agree the next review and the owner of every open action.

## Expansion decision

Recommend another workload/service only if current value is evidenced, the new need is documented, risks are understood and incremental costs are acceptable. Involve a specialist for technical suitability. Additional cloud consumption alone is not a success criterion.
