01 / Discovery

One customer. A focused pilot.

Scenario assumptions

The problem

A small e-commerce support team relies on scattered instructions and repeated status questions.

The pilot

One shared information and status portal. No real orders, payments or production shop migration.

Your role

Understand goals, guide onboarding, coordinate technical help and follow through on open actions.

Success criteria
  1. Verify the deployed portal and endpoint.
  2. Recover from a controlled incident and communicate clearly.
  3. Complete a status, runbook and escalation walkthrough.

How the training score works

Ratings: 0 = blocked, 1 = partial, 2 = criterion met. Sum each weight × rating / 2. Below 50: at risk; 50 to below 80: needs attention; 80+: healthy. Any unknown dimension prevents a numeric score. A recorded critical blocker overrides the label to at risk. These are learning thresholds, not a validated predictor.

Customer health never changes when you run the technical status check. The example is synthetic and is not saved as project evidence.
